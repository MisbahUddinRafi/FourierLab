Counter-Intuitive Insight: For natural and medical images, phase carries most of the recognizable geometric structure, while magnitude carries global contrast. In audio signals, the exact opposite holds: magnitude (spectrogram) dominates human perception and timbre, while phase changes are largely imperceptible.

Understanding the Metrics

MSE (Mean Squared Error): Average squared pixel-by-pixel difference. Lower is better (0 = identical). Treats every pixel independently with no awareness of anatomical structure.

PSNR (Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio): Logarithmic decibel transform of MSE: PSNR = 10 · log10(1 / MSE). Higher is better. A reconstruction above 30 dB is generally considered high quality.

SSIM (Structural Similarity Index): Evaluates local luminance, contrast, and structural correlation using a Gaussian sliding window. Ranges in [-1, 1], where 1.0 is exact match. Unlike MSE, SSIM penalizes blurring and edge loss heavily—capturing visual degradation that humans notice immediately.
